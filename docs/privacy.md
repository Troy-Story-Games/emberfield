# Emberfield privacy policy

*Last updated: October 30, 2026*

Emberfield is a free, open source particle visualizer made by Troy Story Games. This policy covers the Emberfield app on Android, Windows and Linux.

## The short version

Emberfield does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts. On Android it doesn't request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## Microphone and playback audio

The Audio mode can listen to your microphone, or (on Android 10 and newer) to audio that other apps play on your device.

- Audio is processed in memory, on your device, to measure loudness and frequencies for the animation.
- Audio is never recorded, saved to a file, or transmitted.
- The microphone runs only while the Audio mode is selected with Microphone as the source. It stops when you switch modes or leave the app.
- Playback capture runs only while the Audio mode is selected with Playback as the source. Android asks for your permission each time it starts and shows a notification for as long as it runs. It stops when you switch modes, choose another source, or close the app.

## Settings stored on your device

Emberfield saves your settings (mode, colors, sliders) in a small file inside the app's private storage. It never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Children

Emberfield is made for all ages, including young children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Two things sit behind a grown-up check (a short math question): choosing Playback as the audio source, and the link to the source code, which opens a web browser. Microphone mode is open to everyone.

## Changes
